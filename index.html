Findings
  • Margin improved company-wide from 10.23% (2016) to 12.72% (2019) as sales grew from ~$484K to ~$732K.
  • Furniture generated $737K in sales but only a 2.34% margin — versus ~17% for Technology and Office Supplies — the widest sales-to-profit gap in the portfolio.
  • Discount bands above 20% were associated with negative aggregate profit, reaching -119.20% margin at 50%+ — a discount policy flag, not a proven cause.
  • West was the strongest region by margin (14.94%); Central the weakest (7.92%).

Findings
  • Of 149K total bookings, 62.51% completed successfully while 56K (37.5%) were unsuccessful — split between 38K cancelled and 9K incomplete.
  • Auto dominates vehicle demand with 37K bookings and 12.88M in booking value — nearly 2x the next closest vehicle type (Go Mini, 30K bookings).
  • Khandsa, Barakhamba Road, and Saket lead pickup locations, each with 920–949 bookings — a tight cluster of top-performing zones.
  • Built a star-schema data model (Fact_Bookings linked to Dim_Customer, Dim_Vehicle, Dim_Date, dim_pickup, dim_drop) to support drill-down analysis by vehicle type, location, and month.
